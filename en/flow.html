How to play

1. Draw one continuous path from S (Start) to G (Goal). Move up, down, left or right.
2. Visit every cell exactly once.
3. Turn 90° at every gold ◆. Go straight through every blue ●, vertically or horizontally.

◆ Turn here● Go straight
The first three puzzles ease you in. Solve a puzzle, then choose “Next puzzle”. Drag your finger or tap an adjacent cell. You can retrace your steps. “How to think about it” offers general solving advice. Your progress is saved in this browser.
